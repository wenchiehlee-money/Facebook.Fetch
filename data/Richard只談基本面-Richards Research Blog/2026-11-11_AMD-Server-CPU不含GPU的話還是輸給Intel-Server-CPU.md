# AMD Server CPU不含GPU的話，還是輸給Intel Server CPU

原文連結: https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=pfbid024NGCo2YqgYebvLT4DJCrZx3mn9u8Lb2DyjE6hP3xmFrvkzFnCFCyK3Q6DTeEJqXtl&id=100054201473657
AMD Server CPU不含GPU的話，還是輸給Intel Server CPU
但明年成長率遠高於Intel應可追上

------------"AMD vs Intel Analysis

AMD is guided to out-revenue Intel in Q4: ~$17B vs Intel’s ~$15.5B midpoint. Even AMD’s low end beats Intel’s high end, and the gap is almost entirely data centre.

AMD’s data center lead over Intel comes from AI (GPUs and racks), not CPUs. AMD DC was $8.5B vs Intel DCAI at $7.3B in Q3, but take out Instinct and Intel is still bigger in server. Intel’s DCAI grew 78% YoY, its strongest growth in 15 years, with Granite Rapids on Intel 3 ramping fast and Clearwater Forest on 18A shipping for revenue.

In 2026, AMD is holding server CPU share rather than taking it rapidly. 2027 is the key year: Intel expects server CPU units to grow more than 30%, while AMD guides server revenue to roughly double, mostly from units. That points to meaningful x86 share gains for AMD.

On margins, Intel’s DCAI operating margin (41.5%) beats AMD’s DC margin (34%), but AMD’s is diluted by HBM-heavy Instinct GPUs; AMD’s server CPU margin alone likely tops Intel’s. At the company level, AMD’s 56% gross margin vs Intel’s 44% (38% guided for Q4) reflects AMD’s fabless model against Intel Foundry losses.

Both companies are supply-constrained, but differently. Intel expects shortages through 2027 into 2028, is short on Intel 3 wafers and substrates, and is absorbing the Fab 28 incident. AMD relies on TSMC and has secured supply for 2027’s >80% growth.
......
CPU racks and head nodes: The new Helion rack holds up to 144 Venice CPUs and ~74k threads, the first CPU-only rack built for agents, with first systems shipping in Q4. AMD also gets paid on the custom-chip path: OpenAI is using Turin as the host CPU for its ASIC, and several model developers and clouds have signed head-node deals that ramp over the coming quarters."
