AMD財報會議中，有兩點是個人有興趣的

1.前幾天提到Nvidia CUDA軟體障礙被打破，因為目前的AI coding技術更容易將CUDA程式碼軟換或重寫基於AMD ROCm或其他ASIC的程式碼，這點AMD有提到，我想AMD用戶透過AI coding更容易學習、建置ROCm軟體，或移轉porting以前自己累積的CUDA軟體，是對的，但是CUDA的軟體堆疊並非只有如此，還有龐大的CUDA工具軟體和垂直領域軟體，這部分其他陣營可以透過AI coding加速開發，Nvidia自己也可以透過AI coding加速CUDA軟體深度和廣度，各方同時使用AI武器之下，又回到各自基本軟體能力和人力產能等競爭力問題了，整體講，我認同CUDA障礙被降低，但不是全部消失

我們上個月推出了 ROCm.ai，這是我們為 AMD GPU 推出的新 AI 輔助開發平台。ROCm.ai 讓開發者使用現今領先的程式代理程式，包括 Claude、Codex 和 Cursor，來為 Instinct 創建、移植及優化程式碼，讓將新模型與工作負載帶入 AMD 的速度與工作量大幅提升。ROCm.ai 在多種模型中，訓練效能是 ROCm 7 的兩倍以上，且推理效能是 ROCm 7 的三倍。我們也與領先的 AI 實驗室密切合作，包括 OpenAI、Anthropic、Meta 等，共同優化 ROCm 以符合其模型，並帶來的改進惠及整個 AMD 生態系統。
Turning to our AI software stack. ROCm has reached an important inflection point with the performance, capabilities and developer experience customers need to deploy AI in production at scale. The breadth of the ecosystem also continues to expand. More than 3 million models now run out of the box on AMD, the leading open models launched with day 0 support for Instinct and open source contributions to ROCm have increased more than tenfold over the past year. We introduced ROCm.ai, our new AI-assisted development platform for AMD GPUs last month. ROCm.ai let developers use today's leading coding agents, including Claude, Codex and Cursor to create, port and optimize code for Instinct, making it significantly faster and easier to bring new models and workloads to AMD. ROCm.ai delivers more than twice the training performance and won 3x the inferencing performance of ROCm 7 across a broad range of models. We are also working closely with the leading AI labs, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and others to co-optimize ROCm for their models with the improvements benefiting the entire AMD ecosystem.

接著問一個技術性問題。從 Helios 的規格來看，我認為它的重力比競爭對手高出近 50%。而且我覺得你的產品裡一直都有比較多的 HBM。那麼，這有多少好處是因為多了 50% 的 HBM，這會不會讓 AMD 更容易面臨記憶體成本膨脹？所以我很好奇，你們是怎麼確保能夠維持邊界，並且獲得這個記憶體分配的？我想 Jean 已經說過你對毛利率範圍感到滿意。但我很好奇，這有多少好處只是因為多用了 HBM？這是否仍能幫助您達成獲利目標與長期配置要求？
And for my follow-up, a little of a technical question. When we look at the specs of Helios, it has, I think, almost 50% more HBM than the competition. And I think you have always had more HBM in your products. So how much of the benefit is because of having 50% more HBM and then doesn't that expose AMD more to memory cost inflation. So I'm just curious how are you ensuring that you're able to maintain margins, right, and get this memory allocation? And I think Jean already suggested you are comfortable with the gross margin range. But I'm curious how much of the benefit is just because of the use of more HBM? And does that still enable you to meet your profitability targets and allocation requirements over time?
