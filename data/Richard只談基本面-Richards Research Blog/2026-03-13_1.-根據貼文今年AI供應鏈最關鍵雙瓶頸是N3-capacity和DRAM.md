# 1. 根據貼文，今年AI供應鏈最關鍵雙瓶頸是N3 capacity和DRAM

原文連結: https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=pfbid02rMXX8tpbdMX3eRAWD9KATFLsAVe4brEyzpVPaVKceTKvPyA9qK5fV1EpG4zZERYXl&id=100054201473657
1. 根據貼文，今年AI供應鏈最關鍵雙瓶頸是N3 capacity和DRAM

2. 2024~2025年AI用N5/N4, 手機用N3, 2026~2027年AI全部擠到N3例如Rubin/Vera/NVSwitch/Spectrum X/Trainium 3, 手機仍在N3，2027年AI繼續成長，手機還無法全部轉N2，讓2026~2027 N3成為瓶頸，DRAM也類似，HBM容量加大、轉HBM4消耗同bits數四倍DRAM wafer產能(HBM3e消耗三倍)、Vera LPDDR5X 1.5TB是Grace 500GB的三倍......

3. 因此，過去兩年，資料中心區域電力和供應鏈CoWoS是關鍵瓶頸，目前需求還是大於產能，但已經不是最缺貨最瓶頸，最後一段意思就是未來兩年最關鍵瓶頸從CoWoS變成N3 wafer capacity，TSMC擴產CoWoS時程會考慮N3的限制無必要過度投資，"CoWoS tight but easing"

----------------Google翻譯
"CoWoS – 緊繃但逐漸放鬆
前端容量現已成為主要瓶頸，CoWoS 的限制逐漸放寬。雖然 CoWoS 有限，台積電的產能規劃仍考量到 N3 限制。若沒有前端晶圓供應支援，台積電無必要過度投資 CoWoS 產能。2.5D 封裝還有其他選項。CoWoS 過去曾外包給 ASE/SPIL 與 Amkor 等 OSAT。例如，當 Nvidia 宣布出口許可後，曾向 Amkor 包裝運往中國的 H200。Intel 旗艦 EMIB 2.5D 先進封裝解決方案也是另一個日益受到重視的選項，Trainium 與 TPU 採用程度各異。

CoWoS – Tight but Easing
Front-end capacity is now the dominant bottleneck, with CoWoS constraints easing. While CoWoS is limited, capacity planning by TSMC is with N3 constraints in mind. There is no point for TSMC to over-invest in CoWoS capacity if there is no front-end wafer supply to support it. There are also other options for 2.5D packaging. CoWoS can and has previously been outsourced to OSATs like ASE/SPIL and Amkor. For example, Nvidia went to Amkor to package H200s destined for China when the news that export licenses would be granted. Intel’s flagship EMIB 2.5D advanced packaging solution is also another option that is increasingly gaining traction, with Trainium and TPU adopting it to different degrees.""
