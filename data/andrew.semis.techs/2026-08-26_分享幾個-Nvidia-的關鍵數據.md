# 分享幾個 Nvidia 的關鍵數據

原文連結: https://www.facebook.com/andrew.semis.techs/posts/pfbid02vYfy5FkHewPKSmZa6vrKd7MwQt1rpj1t1tn7ktJLFoN1NtQWzwoBy1k6v7uHKcrrl
分享幾個 Nvidia 的關鍵數據

2Q26 sales of US$96.2bn, 18% qoq and 106% yoy, 4% better than Bloomberg est. 75% gross margin was in line with est. 2Q26 EPS of US$2.46 was better than Blmg’s US$2.15. 

2Q26 Data center sales of US$89bn, up 18% qoq and 117% yoy, with 4% better. However, hyperscaler sales of US$48.7bn, up 29% qoq and 104% yoy, was 12% better than blmg’s est.

3Q26 sales guidance of US$105.84-110.16bn, up 10-14% qoq and 89% yoy, is 5% better than market forecast. Gross margin guidance of 73.5-74.5% is slightly lower Blmg’s 74.8%.

4Q26 gross margin will go test 71-72%, much worse than Blmg’s 74.7%.

Nvidia expects fiscal 2028 (Jan 31, 2027-Jan 31, 2028) sales growth of 70% yoy, 25% higher than Bloomberg’s 44.8% yoy.

老實說 Nvidia 的短期數字除了2H26 毛利率受到DRAM/NAND漲價影響一點外，其他沒啥好挑剔的，加上公司補了一句2027還有70%增長（請參考之前報告：2027年初漲價超過15%是為了保毛利率，不光反應成本），亞馬遜加單200萬顆 GPU，盤後股價由跌翻漲。
